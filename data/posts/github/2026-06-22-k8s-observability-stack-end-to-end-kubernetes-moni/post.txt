Spent yesterday chasing down why our Prometheus scrape interval kept drifting under load. We're running a Flask app with custom metrics, scraping every 30s via ServiceMonitor, and the dashboards looked fine until traffic spiked. Then gaps everywhere.

Turns out the issue wasn't Prometheus itself. Our Flask app dropped metrics during garbage collection. We had `process_gc_collections_total` firing but not the actual business metrics we cared about. The ServiceMonitor was working perfectly, but there was nothing to scrape half the time.

What got me was how invisible this was. Prometheus happily recorded whatever came back, Grafana filled the blanks with interpolation, and everything looked smooth. I only caught it because I was staring at the raw scrape output and noticed the timestamps clustering.

Fixed it by adjusting our Flask metrics decorator to use a background thread instead of blocking on the request. But I'm still not confident we've got it right. We're seeing maybe 2-3% dropped samples now instead of 15%, but I can't tell if that's acceptable or if we need a completely different approach to metrics collection under load.

Anyone else deal with metrics loss in custom instrumentation? Wondering if we should be pushing metrics instead of scraping, or if there's a pattern I'm missing with how Flask handles concurrent metric writes.
