Spent the last few days wrestling with why my custom Flask metrics weren't showing up in Prometheus, even though the `/metrics` endpoint was returning data when I curl'd it directly.

Turns out the ServiceMonitor was fine. The scrape config was fine. But I'd set `honor_labels: true` globally in my Prometheus config, and one of my Flask apps was already exporting a `job` label that conflicted with Prometheus's own labeling. So the scrape was happening, metrics were being collected, but Prometheus silently dropped them during the relabel phase.

Caught it by digging through the scrape debug page and looking at the "dropped metrics" section, something I'd never actually checked before. I always assumed if metrics weren't in Prometheus, the scrape itself had failed.

The fix was a single relabel rule to drop that conflicting label before ingestion. What stuck with me though: Prometheus will happily scrape and then discard data without making noise about it. The metrics endpoint looks healthy. The ServiceMonitor looks correct. But stuff just vanishes.

Now I wonder how many times I've had metrics silently dropped in production because I wasn't actively checking that dropped metrics counter. I'm still not sure if there's a better way to surface these issues, or if this is just how Prometheus's label handling works.
